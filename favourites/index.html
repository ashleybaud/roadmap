Founder's fav's

The places I'd send you to, and the trips and races behind them. Twenty-odd years of riding and driving through Southern Africa and Europe. Every place has how to get there, where to sleep, and a button that plans the trip for you.

Namibia

The Orange River roadRosh Pinah to Noordoewer, southern NamibiaMy favourite piece of road anywhere. I've said it twice now, so it must be true.Epupa FallsKunene River, far north-west NamibiaPalm trees, a river the colour of tea, and Angola just across the water.Canyon RoadhouseNear Fish River Canyon, southern NamibiaOld cars, cold beer, middle of nowhere. My kind of church.Sendelingsdrif pontOrange River border crossing, RichtersveldA border crossing on a pont that runs when the river lets it.Oranjemund and the diamond coastOrange River mouth, south-west NamibiaA town that was off-limits for most of its life, at the mouth of the river.Klein-Aus Vista and the desert horsesAus, on the edge of the NamibWild horses living on the edge of a desert, and a lodge in the mountains above them.Sesriem and the TsauchabGateway to Sossusvlei, Namib DesertThe doorway to the big red dunes, and a little canyon most people walk straight past.Skeleton Coast, Torra Bay to Henties BayAtlantic coast, north-west NamibiaFog, shipwrecks and 200 km between fuel. You'll love it or hate it.Grootberg LodgeKlip River valley, DamaralandA lodge on the lip of an escarpment, with a driveway that earns the view.OpuwoKunene Region, north-west NamibiaThe last real town before the far north. Fuel, food, and Himba country all around.Etosha from NamutoniEastern Etosha National ParkA white salt pan the size of a small country, and animals that queue for the waterholes.
