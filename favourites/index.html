Founder's fav's

The places I'd send you to. Twenty-odd years of riding and driving through Southern Africa and Europe, boiled down to the ones I still talk about. Some are roads, some are waterfalls, some are race tracks. Each one has how to get there, where to sleep, and a button that plans the trip for you.
