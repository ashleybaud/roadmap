From the garage archive, 2019.

We decided to take the Flatnose and 996 for an outing to clear our minds and get some video footage. “Pure emotion”, firing up the 996, reversing out the garage, slowly crawling down the street allowing mechanicals to warmup, imaging Im a race driver taking my spot on the grid. What a way to start everyday, it never gets old.
