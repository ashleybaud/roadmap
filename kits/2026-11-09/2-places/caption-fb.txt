One of the highest single-drop waterfalls in Africa, in the mountains of Lesotho.

Maletsunyane Falls drops nearly 200 metres in one go into a gorge outside Semonkong, a small mountain town where people still ride ponies wrapped in blankets. The road in climbs through passes and villages that feel a world away from South Africa, even though they're next door.

Tip: Bring warm, waterproof clothes, even in summer.
