Tuesday 3 Nov · Places

Valentino Rossi's home town, where the whole village is painted yellow. Tavullia is a small hill town near Pesaro that is entirely devoted to its most famous son. The flags are yellow, the speed limit signs say 46, and there's a fan club shop and café that feels like a shrine. Down the road is the ranch where Rossi trains on dirt. Tip: The fan club café is the place to start. Route there from wherever you are, link in bio. 📍 Tavullia and the VR46 MotoRanch, Pesaro #roadtrip #placestogo #onetrackmind
