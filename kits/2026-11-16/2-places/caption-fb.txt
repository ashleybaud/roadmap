The home of Ferrari: museum, factory tour, and test drives in the town.

Maranello is Ferrari's town. The Museo Ferrari has the cars, the trophies and the simulator, the factory tour runs past the Fiorano test track, and outside a dozen shops will rent you a Ferrari for a fifteen-minute drive in the hills.

Tip: The museum shuttle links Maranello and the Enzo Ferrari museum in Modena.
