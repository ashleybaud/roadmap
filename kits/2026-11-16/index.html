Tuesday 17 Nov · Places

The home of Ferrari: museum, factory tour, and test drives in the town. Maranello is Ferrari's town. The Museo Ferrari has the cars, the trophies and the simulator, the factory tour runs past the Fiorano test track, and outside a dozen shops will rent you a Ferrari for a fifteen-minute drive in the hills. Tip: The museum shuttle links Maranello and the Enzo Ferrari museum in Modena. Route there from wherever you are, link in bio. 📍 Maranello, Modena #roadtrip #placestogo #onetrackmind

Thursday 19 Nov · Throwback

Nov 2017. After our first visit to Ducati in ten years, we explored the back roads to Venice. More dual way single track. Very Italian. Learned to trust the GPS, quite refreshing. Although it does not give you the bigger picture, it allows one to be adventurous without getting completely lost! Love the way the traffic just flows without any hooting or drama.
