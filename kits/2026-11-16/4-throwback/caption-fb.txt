Nov 2017.

After our first visit to Ducati in ten years, we explored the back roads to Venice. More dual way single track. Very Italian. Learned to trust the GPS, quite refreshing. Although it does not give you the bigger picture, it allows one to be adventurous without getting completely lost! Love the way the traffic just flows without any hooting or drama. Parking in Venice completely full, so we had to train in.
