Die Hel (Gamkaskloof)

Western Cape · Swartberg · 1 day · Motorbike, 4x4 · Road less travelled

Out and back from Prince Albert over the top of the Swartberg Pass and down the long, steep zigzags of the Elands Pass into Gamkaskloof. A dead end, so you ride it twice. Allow a full day, carry water and don't try it after heavy rain.
