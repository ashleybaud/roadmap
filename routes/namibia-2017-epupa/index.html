Destination Epupa Falls

Namibia · Fish River to Kunene · 7 days · Motorbike, 4x4 · Road less travelled

Ridden by the founder in 2017 · KTM 1290 Super Adventure, with 990, 1190 and an Africa Twin

Canyon Village, Sesriem, Swakopmund, Grootberg and north to Epupa Falls on the Angolan border, back via Opuwo and Windhoek. Sand, corrugations and long gaps between fuel.

The story

A sandstorm on day one, a broken arm on day two, a tyre change by committee in the middle of nowhere and an oil light 180 km from Angola. Textbook how not to adventure bike, which is exactly why we ran bootcamps. We still made Epupa Falls. When I stopped at Opuwo, a lodge owner I'd never met drove me and the bike to Oshakati and sorted the rest over a braai. Namibia does that. It was my father's birthday at the falls. Couldn't think of a better place to spend it.

Good to know

  • Fill up at every station you pass north of Henties Bay.
  • Carry a satellite phone. Cell signal is good in towns, nothing in between.
  • Grootberg Lodge: 4x4 or motorbike for the driveway.
  • Heed the oil light. Nothing good comes from pushing on.
