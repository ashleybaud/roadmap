Windhoek to Sesriem

Namibia · 2 days · Motorbike, 4x4, Car · Road less travelled

Down off the Khomas Highland to Solitaire for the night, then on to Sesriem and the dunes. Gravel all the way after Windhoek. Tyre pressures down, speeds down: corrugations and loose shoulders cause most accidents here.
