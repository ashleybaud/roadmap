The story

The day of avoidance: dogs, pigs, goats, cows, horses, taxis, drunk taxis, potholes and ruts, on a roadbook from a local in Port St Johns. Rain had washed the gravel into ruts. I tried to ride places I'd never been, ran low on fuel looking for the Kei pont, and snuck a lap of the East London GP circuit. Missed the Baviaans turn lost in my thoughts and took the Langkloof instead. Last night at Buffelsdrift with an owner who'd built it for conservation. If your job is stressful, ride shorter days and stop more.

Good to know

  • Livestock and taxis on every road in the old Transkei. Ride slower than you think.
  • Fuel range lulls you. Fill up before hunting for the Kei mouth pont.
  • Day 1 from Durban isn't in the blog; the story starts at Port St Johns.
