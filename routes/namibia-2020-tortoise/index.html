Northern Namibia in the Tortoise

Namibia · Etosha, Kunene, Skeleton Coast · 8 days · 4x4, Car · Road less travelled

Ridden by the founder in 2020 · Toyota 4x4 camper (the Tortoise), two-up

Ten days in a rented 4x4 camper: Etosha, Opuwo, Epupa Falls, Palmwag and down the Skeleton Coast to Walvis. Washed-away roads and blind river crossings after the rains.

The story

The trip started with an expired passport at the check-in counter and only got better. Ten days in a rented camper with Logistics, no fixed plan and a paper map. Etosha after the rains, puddles deep enough to lose a number plate. Back to Opuwo, where the 2017 bike trip fell apart. Five hours for 180 km to Epupa, river crossings washed away, a croc in the reeds. Then Sesfontein, Palmwag, a puncture at 40 degrees and the Skeleton Coast, which my father always talked about and never saw. Not the same as a bike. Flexible, comfortable, and it still clears the mind.

Good to know

  • Gondwana Card: South Africans save up to half on Gondwana lodges.
  • Epupa: don't swim in the river. Crocodiles.
  • Fill up at Torra Bay junction; fuel is far apart on the coast.
  • Two nights at Epupa. The planner shows it as one stop; add a rest day yourself.
