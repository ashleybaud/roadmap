CDBA Day2: Louvain Guest Farm

Lovely to see how entrenched Louvain Guest Farm are in the well being and development of their local community and surrounds. The world needs more people like them!

Awesome days riding, making new friends, with privileged access to special places. All kinds of bikes out there today, everyone having fun, respecting the environment.

General eventually having his 1000km service performed onsite by master technician Wynando Adeleo Donaggi. What took you so long Martin Jones, you need to ride more!
