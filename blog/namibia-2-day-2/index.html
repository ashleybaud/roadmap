Namibia 2, Day 2.
Not much to report today, besides another little meltdown by the General over the accomodation bill because Riaan is still waiting on his Gondwana Card.

Some sightseeing around the fish river canyon, Canyon Roadhouse, Seeheim (great to see almost no signs of the fire damage) and then the straightest, hottest, boring tar stretch to Klein Aus.
Temps reached a high of 41 degrees and tyre pressure increased to almost 2.8 front and 3.6 rear.... admittedly not at recommended speed limits. Only one way to cover horrible distance. Quickly. 1290 Super Adventure made for this stuff!

Somehow lose Riaan and Brian, Again!

Looking forward to day 3 ride back down to Felix Unite along the Orange River.

More photos coming soon.
