A planning aid, not a guarantee

That information can be missing, out of date or wrong. Roads close, wash away, change surface and get regraded. Fuel runs out. Places shut or change hands.

Check everything that matters before you go and on the way: road and border conditions, fuel, accommodation, permits, weather and park rules. Phone ahead on remote legs.

Not for navigation

Don't follow the planner or its GPX files blindly. Obey road signs, the law and local advice. Turn back when the road, the weather or your gut says so. Never use the app while riding or driving.
