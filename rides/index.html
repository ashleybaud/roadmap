The founder's rides

Where it all started. Twenty-odd years of trips through Southern Africa and Europe on bikes, in 4x4s and the odd hired Fiat: the good roads, the wrong turns and the nights we didn't plan. Every one of them opens in the planner, so you can take it, change it and ride it yourself.

These are memories: roads change, places close. Check conditions before you go, and send a ride report so the next rider knows.
