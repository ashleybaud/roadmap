Plan it. Book it. Ride it.

Forget BaseCamp and five different apps. Route a multi-day trip in minutes, sort your beds, then send it straight to your GPS, your bike's app or your phone and navigate from there.

  1. Route it in minutesReal roads, graded for your bike, 4x4 or car. Fuel gaps, sunset and weather checked for every day.
  2. Book your bedsPlaces to stay at every night stop. Pick one, book it, tick it off.
  3. Load your GPSOne tap: the right GPX for Garmin, BMW, KTM, Ducati or Harley.
  4. Or just ride with your phoneEach day opens in Google Maps or Waze. No GPS? No problem.

How it works▾

1. Start

  • New starts a fresh trip (with Undo). Open loads a pick, a rider's route or one you saved. GPX plans from a file. 🧭 Near me shows what's around you.
  • Set From, Finish, your dates and what you like to Stay in. Each day gets a tab.
  • More than one day? Your night stops are picked for you: the trip is split into even days, each night in a town on the way. Change any of them; yours are kept.
  • ✏ Draw lets you tap the map point to point, following roads or in a straight line for tracks the map doesn't show.

2. Stops

  • Your stops have a green bar. SUGGESTED stops are fuel the app adds; ✕ removes one for good.
  • 🛏 Night stops are boxed. "town" means you haven't picked a place to sleep yet.
  • Tap a stop to see it on the map and what's there. Every stop shows the icons you've switched on under On the map (fuel, places to stay, food, sights) on its main line: tap one to zoom to the stop and see just those (faded means none). ⋯ renames, moves or removes it.
  • Press and hold the map (click on a computer) to add a stop, swap one, or sleep there.

3. Roads

  • Each stretch shows how you get there: brown is your road choice from Settings, blue (the freeway) is the quickest way by the main roads, the same as normal sat-nav. Tap it to switch. On Tar every stretch is the freeway.
  • Tar (in Settings) routes like normal sat-nav: main roads, no gravel. Road less travelled finds gravel, not passes: tap Ride it under Passes nearby.
  • Grades, easy to hard: Race · Pre-jeep · Jeep · Pre-single · Single. Read for what you ride: Jeep is fun on a bike, slow in a car.

4. Fuel

  • Set how far you go between fills, with a safe margin (Stop at least every in Settings). It starts at 200 km for a bike and 450 km for a car or 4x4. A fuel town is suggested only when the next stretch is longer than that, and you're assumed to start each day with a full tank.
  • Check fuel means a stretch longer than that with no fuel on our map. Tap it to see where. Tank goes further? Change the setting.

5. Where you sleep

  • Each night lists places to stay, filtered by what you chose (camping, guesthouse…). Tap Info for details, Stay here to choose.
  • Mark booked when it's done. The Overview lists every night with contacts and how many are booked.

6. Near me

  • Tap 🧭 to see routes, passes, fuel, food and stays around you. What shows follows the On the map buttons; zoom in for more.
  • Tap anything: Go here, Add as a stop or There and back. Tap 🧭 again to go back to your trip.

7. Before and on the road

  • Each day shows km, riding time, sunset, weather and climb. Orange means look again.
  • Good to know lists what to sort first: borders, holidays, long gaps without fuel or a hospital.
  • Set My bike or GPS in Settings and the GPX file is made for it (each day, or the whole trip). No GPS? Google Maps / Waze opens each day on your phone, and Send these links sends them to you or the group. Share this trip (or WhatsApp this trip) makes one link anyone can open, no account needed: they get the whole plan, then Google Maps, Waze or GPX. Leading a group? Group ride pack zips every format, phone links included, with a read-me. Or Save as PDF. Sign in to save routes and open them on your phone.

8. Built by riders

  • + Add yours shares a road or marks a place. 📍 on the map marks it in two taps on the road.
  • Report the road, rate a route you rode. We check everything before other riders see it.

Worth knowing

  • Map data can be wrong out in the sticks. Phone ahead on remote stretches, and tell us with Send feedback. Terms

Terrain grades

Graded from what OpenStreetMap says about each road. Roads change after rain and grading, so check locally. Riders' reports will sharpen this.

We show where fuel is; we don't track your tank. Suggested stops use your "Stop at least every" setting. Fuel, places and hospitals come from OpenStreetMap and can be wrong. Phone ahead on remote legs.

Navigate on your phone

Google Maps opens each day with its stops. Waze goes one stop at a time. Both pick their own roads between points: for gravel and back roads, the GPX in your GPS or in OsmAnd, Calimoto or Kurviger follows the line exactly.

What will you load this into?

Each bike app and GPS wants the file a little differently. We'll remember your choice (change it any time in Settings).

Plan a trip, day by day

Pick where you start, your dates and where you sleep each night. The app works out the route, fuel stops, sunset, weather and what to watch for, for every day.

  1. From and dates: where you start, when you leave and when you're back.
  2. Night stops: picked for you on trips of more than one day. Change any you like, and add stops along the way.
  3. Each day: open its tab for fuel stops, what's open when you arrive, and anything to watch for.
  4. Ride it: straight onto your Garmin or bike app as GPX, or open it on your phone in Google Maps or Waze. Plus a PDF to print.

Built by riders: open riders' routes under Find a route, and tap + Add yours to share a road you love or mark a place worth stopping.
