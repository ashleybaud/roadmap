Contributor rules

OneTrackMind is built by riders. When you add a route, mark a place or report the road, other riders plan their trips on it. So:

  1. Only real places and real conditions you've seen yourself.
  2. No adverts, spam or anything offensive.
  3. Don't use your phone while you're riding or driving. Pull over, or let your passenger do it.
  4. Don't mark private land or homes unless they welcome visitors.
  5. We check what you add before other riders see it. We may edit it, refuse it or remove it.
  6. If you break these rules we may block your account from adding anything.
  7. What you add can be shown to everyone with your display name, never your email address.

Questions: hello@onetrackmind.online. See also privacy.
